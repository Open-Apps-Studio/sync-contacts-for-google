Google contacts not syncing on iPhone? Here's what to check

By Open Apps Studio · Updated October 24, 2026

Start by locating one missing contact in Google Contacts on the web. If it is there, investigate the iPhone connection. If it is only in iCloud, you need a transfer or a different save destination, not just a refresh.

1. Check the account and its Contacts setting

Confirm the same Google email address on the web and phone. In Settings → Apps → Contacts → Contacts Accounts, open that Google account and check that Contacts is enabled. On older iOS versions, Contacts may appear directly in Settings.

Open Contacts and look at the Google account under Lists. A filtered view can hide cards that are already available. Check a specific name and number rather than only comparing total counts.

2. Check where new contacts are being saved

If older Google cards appear but newly created iPhone cards do not reach Gmail, inspect the new card’s account. The default account controls the usual destination for new cards; it does not relocate existing ones. Follow the Gmail default-account guide if that is the problem.

3. Resolve account or permission errors

Complete any sign-in or account-access prompts through the normal Google connection. For a third-party app, check both its Google authorization and its iPhone Contacts permission. A work account may also be subject to administrator rules.

Re-adding an account can be a later troubleshooting step, but first confirm that important cards exist on the web and export any unique phone data. Removing an account from a phone and deleting individual cloud contacts are different actions. Read the prompts carefully and avoid removing the only accessible copy.

4. Check connectivity and background expectations

Test on a working connection, open the relevant Contacts or sync app and inspect the result. Low Power Mode can restrict background work. Mail fetch controls are not a guarantee of a contact-update interval; do not promise that a 15-minute mail setting will fix every contact delay.

If a manual app sync also fails, record its error before changing more settings. If foreground sync succeeds but background updates are delayed, use the background sync guide.

Verify the fix with a small test

Make a harmless change to a test card in the intended source, then check the destination. Verify both a new card and an edit. Keep the working configuration stable once the result is clear.
