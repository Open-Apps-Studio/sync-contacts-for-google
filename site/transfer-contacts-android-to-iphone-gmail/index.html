Transfer contacts from Android to iPhone using Gmail

By Open Apps Studio · Updated October 24, 2026

If your Android contacts are already in Google Contacts, add that same Google account to the iPhone and enable Contacts. Check the web copy first: some Android contacts may be saved only to the device or another account.

Step 0: Confirm your contacts are actually in Google

Open Google Contacts and check the email address and several familiar cards. If the list is incomplete, inspect the Android phone’s contact accounts before starting the transfer. Export device-only contacts from the source Contacts app and import them into Google if needed; menus vary by phone.

Keep the original export and the old phone until the new setup is verified. If you have already used a migration tool, check what it copied before importing another full collection.

Add your Google account to the iPhone

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Contacts → Contacts Accounts → Add Account.
  2. Choose Google and sign in to the account containing the contacts.
  3. Enable Contacts and finish setup.
  4. Open Contacts, select the Google list, and check your sample cards.

Allow time for the account to refresh. If the expected cards remain missing, compare the web and phone account addresses and use the sync troubleshooting guide.

Choose where future iPhone contacts should go

The newly connected Google account does not automatically become the destination for all future contacts. Check the default account and decide where you want new personal cards saved. If you choose Gmail, those new cards belong to Google; that is a valid setup, not a failed iCloud transfer.

Use the default-account guide, then create a harmless sample card and confirm it in the intended web account. Existing cards stay where they were unless you move or synchronize them separately.

Check details before retiring the Android phone

Inspect an emergency contact, a recent addition, an international number and a card with several fields. If you see duplicates, determine whether two accounts are being displayed or a migration created overlapping copies. A second full import is rarely a useful first diagnostic step.

Keep both platforms in your workflow

If you need ongoing synchronization between iPhone contacts and Google, review Sync Contacts for Google’s preview and direction controls. If the Google account alone covers your needs, the built-in connection may be sufficient.
