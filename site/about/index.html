About these contact guides

Open Apps Studio makes Sync Contacts for Google and publishes this website to help people manage contacts across iPhone, Google and iCloud.

How we maintain the guides

Our guides explain built-in methods alongside situations where a separate sync app may help. We link to Apple and Google documentation for platform instructions and distinguish a one-time transfer from ongoing synchronization and backup.

These guides were prepared with AI assistance and checked against the cited documentation and the project’s implementation where relevant. They are not a claim that every workflow has been manually tested on every iOS version, account type or device. Menu labels and organization policies can differ.

Each article displays its last substantive update date. We revise instructions when the underlying behavior changes, and use separate pages for tasks with different outcomes. We do not promise search rankings, instant background execution or recovery of permanently deleted data.

Our relationship to the app

We publish these guides and the app they link to. App Store links may contain page-specific campaign tags so eligible referral results can be measured in Apple’s reporting. They do not contain your contacts.

Corrections and support

Use the support page to report a problem or an outdated instruction. Include the page URL and software version; you do not need to send your address book. Read our privacy policy for product data practices.
