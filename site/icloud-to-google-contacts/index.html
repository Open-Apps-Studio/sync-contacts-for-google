How to move iCloud contacts to Google Contacts

By Open Apps Studio · Updated October 24, 2026

To copy iCloud contacts into Google, export the iCloud collection as a vCard and import it into the intended Google account. For ongoing updates, decide separately how the two address books should stay aligned.

Method 1: Export from iCloud, import into Google (manual)

First check that the contacts you want are actually in iCloud. An iPhone can display Google, work and iCloud accounts together. Use a tablet or computer for Contacts on iCloud.com, and keep an export of the destination Google account if it already contains important cards.

  1. Open iCloud Contacts and sign in to the correct Apple Account.
  2. Select the intended contacts. To copy the whole collection, use the Actions menu and Select All Contacts.
  3. Use the Share menu and choose Export vCard. Keep the downloaded .vcf file unchanged.
  4. Open Google Contacts and confirm the destination email address.
  5. Choose Import, select the vCard file and complete the import.

Check several cards in Google before doing anything to the source: a recent contact, one with several phone numbers, and one containing notes. If fields are not where you expect, inspect the full card. Use our import troubleshooting guide if the upload fails.

Limitations of the manual method

  • It is a snapshot. Later changes in iCloud do not update the independent imported copy.
  • Fields can differ between systems. Check photos, labels and notes that matter to you.
  • Repeated imports can overlap. Review what was imported before uploading the same collection again.

You can display the resulting Google contacts on your iPhone by connecting that account. That is different from keeping the original iCloud cards and the Google copies synchronized.

Method 2: Choose an ongoing sync workflow

If you need changes to move between the address books, choose a sync direction and review what the first pass will change. Keep an independent export before connecting a new workflow. Do not assume that changing the iPhone default account moves older cards.

Review before you sync

Sync Contacts for Google offers a first-sync preview and direction controls. Check the proposed additions and updates before confirming. Background timing depends on iOS and connectivity; open the app and check the result for an urgent change.
