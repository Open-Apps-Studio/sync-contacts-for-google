How it works

Set it up once in under a minute

Three steps, then it's automatic. No account to create — you sign in with the Google account you already have.

  1. Sign in with Google

    Pick the Google account you want to sync with. The app requests contacts access only — no location, no tracking permissions.

  2. Preview the first sync

    See exactly what will happen — contacts added, updated, and matched on each side — before a single change is written.

  3. Forget about it

    From then on the app keeps both sides in sync automatically. New contacts, edits, photos, and deletions flow both ways.

Honest answer: how fast is “background” sync?

iOS doesn't let any app run non-stop in the background — including the expensive ones. Here's what actually happens: when something changes in your Google Contacts, our server sends your phone a silent push that wakes the app to sync. Combined with iOS background refresh, changes typically sync within the hour, and always instantly the moment you open the app. Any app that promises more than that on iOS is overselling.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the app really free? What's the catch?

Yes — free forever, for all your contacts. No subscription, no 40-contact cap, no ads, no features held hostage. We build small utilities at Open Apps Studio and keep this one free; our server costs are tiny precisely because your contacts never pass through our servers.

How does background sync actually work on iOS?

iOS doesn't let apps run permanently in the background, so we're honest about timing: changes typically sync within the hour via silent push wake-ups and iOS background refresh, and always sync instantly when you open the app.

Do my contacts pass through your servers?

No. Sync happens directly between your device and Google's People API. Our server only sends anonymous wake-up pushes and stores zero personal data — no contacts, no names, no emails, no Google credentials. See the privacy policy for the full details.

What happens on the very first sync?

You get a full preview before anything changes: how many contacts will be added, updated, or merged on each side. Nothing is written until you confirm, and a local backup is taken first.

Can a bad sync delete my contacts?

The app has a deletion guard: if a sync would delete an unusually large number of contacts, it pauses and asks you before proceeding. Automatic local backups are kept so you can restore at any time.

Does it work with Google Workspace accounts?

Yes, as long as your Workspace admin allows third-party access to the Google People API. Personal Gmail accounts work out of the box. More edge cases are covered on the support page.
